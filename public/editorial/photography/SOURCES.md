## Handling notes

- Files were downloaded from the corresponding Unsplash image delivery endpoint and resized by that endpoint to a maximum width of 1800 pixels at quality 82.
- Crops are applied non-destructively in CSS so desktop and mobile framing can be reviewed independently.
- No photograph is intended to show real customer billing data. Screen and paper detail remains incidental and is not used as evidence for site claims.
- The official Stealth Agents team image is not part of this licensed set. Its existing approved source, wording, and attribution remain in the Contact Us template.
